# Cell Test Bench

Single-cell characterization bench for 18650 and 21700 Li-ion cells (DMEGC INR45E, generic 3.5 Ah 18650).
Part of the Solaris final-year project.

- 0–10 A linear electronic load (constant current), down to 2.0 V
- Charging up to 5 A from a 30 V / 10 A bench PSU through an XL4016 CC/CV module, voltage set by a DAC
- Bidirectional current measurement on one 10 mΩ 4-terminal shunt
- Cell pocket held at about 10–45 °C by two Peltiers on an aluminium cradle
- STM32F103 Blue Pill, USB CDC to a Python UI
- Hardware protection (OV, UV, OC, OT) that works without the firmware

## Schematic status: v0.1 draft

The schematic was generated by a script, so it is drawn in "netlist style": every pin has a short wire and a net label rather than routed wires. Connectivity was checked against KiCad's own netlist export. The layout still needs tidying by hand.

- Files are saved in KiCad 7 format. KiCad 10 opens them and asks to upgrade on save.
- Footprints are not assigned yet.
- Modules (Blue Pill, ADS1115, MCP4725, XL4016, BTS7960, buck converters) and the relays are drawn as connector headers. Check the pin order of the modules you buy, because it varies between sellers.
- Inter-sheet signals use global labels. Power nets: `GND`, `+12V`, `+5V`, `+3V3` (from the Blue Pill), `VIN` (30 V), `+12V_SW` (after the thermostat).
- Reference designators are numbered per sheet (R1xx = Power, R2xx = Cell/Relays, R3xx = Load, R4xx = Charger, R5xx = Measurement, R6xx = Protection, R7xx = Thermal, R8xx = MCU).

If you edit the schematic in KiCad, those edits become the source of truth. Do not re-run the generator after that, because it would overwrite them.

## Sheets

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| Power | PSU input, fuse, TVS, 12 V and 5 V buck modules, thermostat cutting `+12V_SW` |
| Cell_Relays | Cell power and Kelvin-sense connectors, cell fuse, 10 mΩ shunt, relays K1 (charger) and K2 (load) with interlock and fault drop-out |
| Electronic_Load | IRFP260N linear CC loop (OPA2188), MCP4725 setpoint, series 0.1 Ω 50 W resistor, kill transistors, bidirectional current monitor `I_MON` |
| Charger | XL4016 CC/CV module, second MCP4725 injecting current into FB to lower the output from a 4.25 V hardware ceiling |
| Measurement | ADS1115 #1 (shunt, cell voltage), ADS1115 #2 (four NTCs), `V_FAST` divider for the internal ADC |
| Protection | TL431 2.5 V reference, LM339 + LM393 comparators, `FAULT_N` wired-OR, `FAULT` inverter |
| Thermal | BTS7960 H-bridge for the Peltiers, fan PWM, pocket mixing fan |
| MCU | Blue Pill headers, USB back-feed diode, debug UART, I2C pull-ups |

## Key design numbers

- Load setpoint: DAC 0–3.3 V → divider 29.4 k / 1 k → 0–108 mV → 0–10.8 A.
- Current monitor: `I_MON = 1.65 V + 0.12 V/A`.
- Protection trips: OV > ~4.29 V, UV < ~1.95 V, discharge > ~11 A, charge > ~6.4 A, heatsink or cell NTC > ~62 °C.
- Charger: set the XL4016 CV pot to 4.25 V before connecting the FB tap. The DAC can only lower the voltage from there.

## Known limits of this draft

- The XL4016 module's own current limit is bypassed, because its IN− and OUT− share system ground. Charge current is regulated by firmware and limited by the OC comparator.
- UV protection also trips when no cell is inserted, and prevents charging a cell that is below ~1.95 V.
- The comparators have no hysteresis yet. The firmware is expected to latch faults.
- Check that the BTS7960 module accepts 3.3 V logic inputs.
