# What changed in v0.4, and why

**Author:** Claude, for Hasan Turan · **Date:** 2026-11-10

v0.4 is the first version meant to be built. Three things drove it:

1. Every part has to be buyable in Türkiye. Several v0.3 parts were not (checked 2026-11-10).
2. The board has to come off the Snapmaker, so it is **single-sided**.
3. The open items in `SCHEMATIC_REVIEW.md` had to be closed. You had not filled in the checklist, so I made the calls below. Each one is easy to reverse if you disagree.

The schematic is generated by `hardware/tools/design.py`. Nobody had edited the v0.3 KiCad files by hand, so v0.4 regenerates them (see "Regenerating" in `README.md`).

## 1. Parts replaced because of availability

| v0.3 part | Problem | v0.4 |
|---|---|---|
| MCP4725 DAC module ×2 | Sold out at Direnc and Robotistan | Removed. Three Blue Pill PWM outputs with RC filters set the load current, the charger voltage and the charger current limit. |
| ADS1115 module ×2 | 145 TL each | One module (shunt + cell voltage). The four NTCs go to the Blue Pill ADC, which is accurate enough for temperature. |
| BTS7960 (IBT-2) | Sold out at Direnc and Motorobit, about 400 TL at Robolink, and its logic wants 5 V (T-01) | Removed. Relay K3 (DPDT) swaps the Peltier polarity and one IRLZ44N switches it with PWM, both on the main board. |
| OPA2188 | No stock at Özdisan | OPA2171AIDR (Direnc). Offset 0.25 mV typical, 1.8 mV max; handled by the bias on INN and by the firmware. |
| Ohmite LVK25 4-terminal shunt | DigiKey only | Isabellenhütte CMS 10 mΩ 2512, 2.5 W (Direnc). The footprint adds two Kelvin taps at the inner pad corners. |
| Keystone 3568 fuse holders | DigiKey only | Inline blade fuse holders in the PSU + lead (5 A) and the cell + lead (15 A). They are off the board. |
| MP1584 5 V module | Not found | L7805CV. The 5 V rail only feeds the comparators now (about 30 mA). |
| BS170 ×10 | 3.3 V gate drive is marginal (R-02) | BC337 / BC547 bipolar transistors. |
| 1206 resistors and capacitors | Hard to route on one copper layer | Through-hole 1/4 W resistors and 5 mm ceramic capacitors. Tracks pass between their pads. |
| JST-XH connectors | Not found in the shops searched | 5.08 mm screw terminals everywhere. |
| P6KE33A | 28.2 V stand-off is below 30 V (P-01); P6KE36A not found | P6KE39CA (bidirectional) plus a 1N5408 across the input for reverse polarity. |
| 1N4007 | Sold out at Direnc | 1N5819 for the relay and fan flyback diodes. |
| 60 mm fans + extruded heatsinks | No suitable heatsink found | Two 80 × 80 × 54 mm CPU coolers with their own 12 V fans (Motorobit). |

## 2. Review items closed

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| P-01 | TVS changed to P6KE39CA (33.3 V stand-off). Reverse polarity: D102 conducts and the inline fuse blows. |
| P-02 | PSU fuse 5 A (the bench draws under 3 A from 30 V). |
| P-04 | 5 V from an L7805CV. The pocket fan is now a 12 V type so the 7805 stays cool. |
| P-05 | The thermostat now sits in series with the coil of safety relay K4. It carries 30 mA instead of 3 A and still cuts `+12V_SW` without firmware. |
| R-01, F-01 | Relay footprint with round holes (`…ALFG_FormA_CircularHoles`), pads enlarged for milling. Still check the pin pitch against a real relay before drilling. |
| R-02 | Coil drivers are BC337, switched fully by 3.3 V through 1 kΩ. |
| R-04 | New 2512 footprint: pads 1/4 carry current, pads 2/3 are the Kelvin taps. |
| R-05, R-06 | Fuses are inline. Cell and load-resistor connectors are 7.62 mm pluggable terminals. |
| L-02 | OPA2171 swings to within 0.3 V of 12 V. The IRFP260N needs about 5 V at 10 A. |
| L-04 | 1 % resistors everywhere. The firmware closes the current loop on the ADS1115 reading, so setpoint errors do not matter. |
| L-07, X-05 | The current monitor offset now comes from VREF (TL431), the same reference as the trip points. |
| L-08 | No DAC EEPROM any more. PWM pins are high-Z at reset, which gives zero load current and zero allowed charge current. |
| L-09 | The load is off by default: `LOAD_OFF` is pulled up to +5 V and the MCU must pull it low. |
| C-01 | **Hardware charge-current limit added** (U401A): it compares `I_MON` with `ILIM` and pulls the XL4016 FB pin. Maximum 5.6 A, set by PWM. |
| C-05 | Unchanged: K1 opens when 12 V drops. |
| M-02 | 1 % divider, calibrate once against a DMM. |
| M-04 | `SENSE_P` is buffered by U401B. Only the ADS1115 divider (20 kΩ, 0.2 mA) still loads the cell. |
| X-01 | Kept ground-referenced (accepted). OV nominal moved to 4.33 V so the shunt drop at 5 A does not cause false trips. |
| X-03 | **Fault latch added** (U602C). A trip holds until the firmware re-arms. Power-up state is "tripped". |
| X-07 | One kill point instead of four: `FAULT_N` low drops safety relay K4, which removes `+12V_SW` from K1, K2, K3 and the Peltier. The load loop is also forced off through D302. |
| T-01 | Gone with the BTS7960. |
| T-02 | The fans are switched by a BC337. The Peltier FET is an IRLZ44N at 3 A. |
| U-01 | JP801 (normally open) separates the board's 5 V from the Blue Pill 5 V pin. The Blue Pill runs from USB. |
| U-04 | I2C pull-ups are on the ADS1115 module. R801/R802 removed. |
| F-02 | `PGND` (power return) and `GND` (signal) are separate nets, joined only by link JP201 at the shunt. |

## 3. Things that are different to build

- **Wire jumpers.** A single copper layer cannot make every connection. The layout uses wire links on the component side (W1, W2 …). `hardware/fab/jumpers.csv` lists them.
- **Two SMD parts** (shunt R201, op-amp U301) are soldered on the copper side. Everything else is through-hole.
- **XL4016 modules:** the 8 A module sold at Motorobit has a voltage trimmer only. That is fine: the board does not use the module's own current limit.
- **Peltier wiring:** two wires from J702 to the two Peltiers in series. No driver board in the thermal unit any more, so the decision of 2026-10-28 (BTS7960 in the thermal unit) no longer applies.
- **Budget:** the 1500 TL target cannot be met at 2026 prices. See `hardware/bom.csv` and `hardware/bom_offboard.csv` for the real total.

## 4. Enclosure changes (mechanical/enclosure.py)

- Load heatsink and hot-side heatsink are both the 80 × 80 × 54 mm CPU cooler with its 12 V fan.
- Electronics box: the load cooler stands on its side in a printed cradle (`E4_cooler_cradle`), base flush with the PCB edge under Q301; 80 mm grille in the front wall; lid ribs hold the cooler down. The XL4016 module plate moved clear of the lid-screw boss (it collided in v0.3).
- Thermal unit: base height 72.8 mm so the cooler stands on four posts above a floor grille; the cold plate is clamped by four countersunk M3 screws into bosses in the base. The BTS7960 standoffs became a wiring chamber.
- Grommets moved to match the new connector positions.
- `mechanical/check.py` checks bed size and part collisions; it is clean.

## 5. Not verified

- Nothing has been built or powered. Follow `docs/BRINGUP.md` step by step.
- The load loop, the charger current loop and the latch are designed by calculation only. No simulation was run.
- The pin order of the ADS1115 module and the footprint of the HK14FD relay must be checked against the parts you receive.
- Stock and prices are from one day. A few generic items (screws, wire, aluminium, fuses) are priced by estimate.
