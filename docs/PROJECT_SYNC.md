## 1. Goal

Build a single-cell Li-ion characterization bench so that, in the end, we can fit a **2RC equivalent-circuit model** of a cell (OCV, R0, R1/C1, R2/C2 versus SOC and temperature).

Order of work: (1) hardware and mechanics (**design done in v0.4**), (2) build and bring-up, (3) firmware + Python UI, (4) test procedures and model fitting.

This is part of the Solaris final-year project (see the DAB converter project).

- **Budget:** about 5,500 TL at 2026-11-10 prices (`hardware/bom.csv` + `hardware/bom_offboard.csv`). The old 1500 TL target cannot be met.
- **Workshop:** hand soldering, FDM printing (PETG/ASA, 220 × 220 mm bed), PCB milling on the Snapmaker Artisan / Artisan Premium.

## 2. What the bench does (requirements)

- **Cells:** 18650 and 21700, one at a time (DMEGC INR45E, generic 3.5 Ah 18650).
- **Discharge:** 0 to 10 A linear constant-current load, down to 2.0 V.
- **Charge:** from a 30 V bench PSU through an XL4016 module. Voltage set by MCU PWM below a 4.25 V hardware ceiling (module trimmer); **hardware current limit** 0–5.6 A set by MCU PWM.
  - Up to 5 A for the 21700 (INR45E).
  - The generic 3.5 Ah 18650 is limited to about 0.5C (≈1.75 A) until its datasheet is known.
- **Measurement:** bidirectional current on one 10 mΩ 2512 shunt with Kelvin taps, Kelvin cell voltage, both on one ADS1115. Four NTCs on the Blue Pill ADC. Fast current and voltage on the Blue Pill ADC for R0.
- **Temperature control:** cell held at about 10 to 45 °C in an insulated pocket, 2 × TEC1-12706, polarity relay for heat/cool.
- **Control:** STM32F103 Blue Pill, USB CDC to a Python UI.
- **Safety:** hardware OV / UV / OC / OT protection, latched, that drops a safety relay (removes power from all relay coils and the Peltier) without firmware. Power-up state is tripped.

## 5. Hard constraints (do not break without agreeing)

1. One cell at a time, 18650 or 21700, swapped by changing the printed saddle.
2. Pocket temperature range about 10 to 45 °C. Printed parts near the cell and the load cooler must be PETG or ASA, not PLA.
3. Every printed part fits a 220 × 220 mm bed (`mechanical/check.py` checks it).
4. Max currents:
   - Discharge: 10 A (trip about 11.6 A).
   - Charge: 5 A (hardware limit 5.6 A, trip about 6.2 A); generic 18650 about 1.75 A.
   - Wiring, contacts and the shunt path must carry this. No wire jumpers in the cell loop.
5. Cell voltage limits enforced in hardware: OV about 4.33 V, UV about 1.96 V. OT trip about 61 °C on the heatsink or cell NTC. Charger ceiling 4.25 V.
6. Cell terminals need four-wire (Kelvin) contact.
7. Hand-solderable parts: through-hole, plus R201 (2512) and U301 (SOIC-8) on the copper side.
8. PCB design rules for milling: 0.5 mm track, 0.4 mm clearance, copper ≥ 1 mm from the edge, annular ring ≥ 0.4 mm.
9. Every part must be buyable in Türkiye (`hardware/tools/sourcing.py` holds shop, price, stock and link).
10. The KiCad files are generated. Once someone edits them by hand in KiCad, those files become the source of truth: don't re-run the generators after that.

## 6. Known limits and risks (carry these forward)

- **Not built yet.** All circuit numbers are calculated, not measured. Follow `docs/BRINGUP.md`.
- **Wire jumpers:** a single copper layer needs several wire jumpers on the component side (`hardware/fab/jumpers.csv`).
- **Milled copper is thin:** reinforce the cell loop and PGND strip with 1.5 mm² wire (`docs/CNC_MILLING.md`).
- **Voltage trips are ground-referenced:** at 5 A charge OV acts near 4.28 V, at 10 A discharge UV near 2.06 V.
- **UV trips with no cell** and blocks arming without a cell (intended).
- **ADS1115 divider** draws 0.2 mA from a cell left in the pocket with the bench off.
- **Check before soldering:** ADS1115 module pin order, HK14FD relay pinout, SRD relay contact pins, real cooler base flatness.
- **Load loop stability** depends on layout: scope the current step at bring-up (C304).
- **3D envelopes:** cooler, XL4016 and resistor shapes are envelopes. Measure real parts and update `mechanical/enclosure.py`.
- **Lowest cell temperature:** depends on the hot-side cooler; measure before relying on below-ambient tests.
- **Prices and stock:** checked on one day (2026-11-10). A few generic items are estimates.

## 8. Change protocol

1. Before changing any number in section 4 or 5, edit this file in the same commit and add a line to the changelog.
2. If hardware moves a connector, heatsink position, or mounting hole, the PCB STEP must be regenerated and the mechanical side told via section 7.
3. If mechanics changes box size, standoff height, or cable path, the hardware side is told via section 7.
4. Start of every chat: read this file, then `README.md` (and `mechanical/README.md` for 3D work).
